Per-optimization ablation for pyflate (scripts/ablation.py).

Each row is the optimized decoder with exactly one change reverted to the
shipped implementation, so the gap to the full optimized row is what that
change is worth. Every variant is checked against the benchmark's own md5
before it is timed, so a row that decoded wrongly could not appear here.

  variant                                          min (ms)     median   vs base
  baseline (as pyperformance ships it)               1111.8     1115.5     1.00x
  optimized (all of 3.1-3.5)                          475.3      476.3     2.34x
  optimized, 3.1 reverted to the table scan           561.7      562.9     1.98x
  optimized, 3.3 reverted to the slice rebuild        478.2      479.2     2.33x

  contribution of each separable change (full optimized = 475.3 ms):
    3.1    worth   86.4 ms, i.e. 1.18x of the overall 2.34x
    3.3    worth    2.9 ms, i.e. 1.01x of the overall 2.34x

  3.2 (bit reader) and 3.5 (local names, bytearray) are inside the decode
  loop and cannot be reverted in isolation; they account for the remainder.

  reps per variant: 5, minimum reported (medians in the table above).
