Per-optimization ablation for pyflate (scripts/ablation.py).

Each row is the optimized decoder with exactly one change reverted to the
shipped implementation, so the gap to the full optimized row is what that
change is worth. Every variant is checked against the benchmark's own md5
before it is timed, so a row that decoded wrongly could not appear here.

  variant                                          min (ms)     median   vs base
  baseline (as pyperformance ships it)               1111.5     1117.0     1.00x
  optimized (all of 3.1-3.5)                          480.8      481.5     2.31x
  optimized, 3.1 reverted to the table scan           563.9      565.1     1.97x
  optimized, 3.3 reverted to the slice rebuild        636.7      639.0     1.75x
  optimized, 3.4 reverted to the per-byte RLE loop      637.4      639.6     1.74x

  contribution of each separable change (full optimized = 480.8 ms):
  These are leave-one-out differences. They do not multiply out to the
  overall speedup and are not meant to: each says what the run costs with
  that one change put back, everything else kept.
  Largest run-to-run spread across variants: 69.9 ms - a contribution
  smaller than that is not resolved by this measurement.
    3.1    worth   83.0 ms, i.e. 1.17x of the overall 2.31x
    3.3    worth  155.8 ms, i.e. 1.32x of the overall 2.31x
    3.4    worth  156.6 ms, i.e. 1.33x of the overall 2.31x

  3.2 (bit reader) and 3.5 (local names, bytearray) are woven through the
  decode loop and cannot be reverted on their own, so they are not listed
  separately; what they are worth is whatever the rows above do not explain.

  reps per variant: 5, minimum reported (medians in the table above).
